LEAD SCREW TYPES AND STANDARDS
────────────────────────────────
  T8   : diameter 8 mm, pitch 2 mm, lead 8 mm (4-start), brass nut
           most common in desktop 3D printers (Z axis)
  T10  : diameter 10 mm, pitch 2 mm, lead 2 mm (1-start) or 4 mm (2-start)
  T12  : diameter 12 mm, pitch 2 mm, lead 4 mm (2-start) or 8 mm (4-start)
  ACME : imperial trapezoidal; 1/4"-16, 3/8"-12, 1/2"-10 (TPI)
  Tr8  : ISO trapezoidal 8 mm, similar to T8 but DIN standard

BALL SCREW STANDARDS (higher precision, lower friction)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  SFU1204 : diameter 12 mm, pitch 4 mm, end machined to ø10 mm, flanged nut
  SFU1605 : diameter 16 mm, pitch 5 mm, end machined to ø12 mm
  SFU2005 : diameter 20 mm, pitch 5 mm, end machined to ø15 mm
  SFU2510 : diameter 25 mm, pitch 10 mm

ANTI-BACKLASH NUT DIMENSIONS (T8 brass — most common)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  T8 standard nut : OD 22 mm, height 10 mm, 4×M3 holes on ø16 mm bolt circle
  T8 anti-backlash: OD 22 mm, total height 34 mm (spring loaded), same bolt circle
  POM nut         : OD ~15 mm, friction nut, no flange — use round pocket

END SUPPORT BEARING OPTIONS
────────────────────────────
  KFL08 (fixed end):   F688ZZ flanged bearing, 8 mm bore, supports axial + radial load
  FK08 / FK10 / FK12:  fixed end machined unit, includes locking collar
  FF08 / FF12:         floating end (radial only, no axial constraint)
  Simple pillow block: 608zz/6201zz bearing in printed housing (see bearing reference)

STEPPER MOTOR TORQUE / LEADSCREW PAIRING
──────────────────────────────────────────
  NEMA17 (40 Ncm)  + T8 8mm lead    → vertical Z axis, slow traverse
  NEMA17 (59 Ncm)  + T8 8mm lead    → vertical fast; or small horizontal
  NEMA23 (1.26 Nm) + T12 / SFU1605  → CNC Z axis, medium load
  NEMA34 (4.0 Nm)  + SFU2005        → heavy CNC Z, router table

KEY PARAMETERS
──────────────
  screw_diameter        — 8, 10, 12, 16, 20 mm
  lead                  — linear advance per revolution (mm)
  nut_od                — from table above (T8: 22 mm)
  nut_height            — from table above (T8: 10 mm)
  nut_bolt_circle       — from table above (T8: 16 mm)
  nut_bolt_count        — 4 standard
  nut_bolt_diameter     — M3 for T8, M4 for T12
  nut_pocket_depth      — nut_height + 0.5 mm
  coupler_shaft_a       — stepper shaft diameter (5 mm NEMA17)
  coupler_shaft_b       — screw diameter (8 mm T8)
  coupler_od            — ≥ max(shaft_a, shaft_b) + 10 mm
  coupler_length        — 25–35 mm
  end_bearing_bore      — screw_diameter (e.g. 8 mm for T8)
  anti_rotation_slot    — for floating end: 1–2 mm slot to absorb thermal growth

COMPLETE T8 Z-AXIS MODULE PARAMETERS
──────────────────────────────────────
  rod_diameter       = 8;      // smooth rod
  rod_spacing        = 40;     // centre-to-centre
  screw_diameter     = 8;      // T8 leadscrew
  screw_offset       = 0;      // screw centre from rod centreline midpoint
  carriage_length    = 40;
  carriage_width     = rod_spacing + 30;
  carriage_height    = 30;
  nut_od             = 22;
  nut_height         = 10;
  nut_bolt_circle    = 16;

OPENSCAD STRUCTURE
───────────────────
  module leadscrew_nut_carriage() {
    difference() {
      cube([carriage_length, carriage_width, carriage_height], center=true);
      // two rod bores (LM8UU: OD 15 mm)
      for(y=[-rod_spacing/2, rod_spacing/2])
        translate([0, y, 0])
          rotate([0,90,0])
            cylinder(h=carriage_length+2, d=15.1, center=true, $fn=64);
      // T8 nut pocket
      translate([0, 0, carriage_height/2 - nut_height])
        cylinder(h=nut_height+0.5, d=nut_od+0.4, $fn=64);
      // nut bolt holes
      for(a=[0,90,180,270])
        rotate([0,0,a])
          translate([nut_bolt_circle/2, 0, carriage_height/2-nut_height-3])
            cylinder(h=nut_height+8, d=3.3, $fn=32);
      // leadscrew through bore
      cylinder(h=carriage_height+2, d=screw_diameter+1.0, center=true, $fn=64);
    }
  }

DESIGN RULES
────────────
  - T8 through-bore in carriage: ≥ screw_diameter + 1.5 mm clearance (not a nut — just clearance)
  - Nut pocket: tight fit OD (nut_od + 0.2–0.4 mm) plus precise depth so nut seats flat
  - Anti-backlash nut: allow 2 mm axial float in pocket; spring does the work
  - For vertical Z axis: no anti-rotation rod needed (motor prevents rotation via coupler)
  - For horizontal axis: add two smooth rods or MGN rail to prevent nut rotation
  - Floating end support: bearing + spring or loose collar; only radial constraint
  - Critical alignment: screw must be coaxial with motor shaft within ±0.5 mm radially

OPENSCAD CODE INTERPRETATION
Mechanical meaning to code:
- A lead screw actuator combines motor mount, screw axis, nut carriage, guide support, and end bearings.
- Brackets, carriage block, nut holder, bearing blocks, and rail supports are solid material in union().
- Screw clearance, nut pocket, bearing bores, rail holes, and mounting holes are removed material in difference().
- The screw axis should be straight and coaxial through motor coupler, nut, and end bearing.
- A nut pocket is a larger recess shaped to hold the lead nut, with screw clearance passing through.
- Guide rods or rails are represented by parallel holes or rail mounting slots.
- Coupler clearance should be centered between motor shaft and screw axis.
- Avoid blocking the moving carriage path with solid geometry.

OPENSCAD CODE RULE IMPLEMENTATIONS
Use these patterns to turn lead-screw actuator rules into OpenSCAD.

Rule: nut carriage is a block with screw clearance through it.
OpenSCAD action:
  difference() {
    cube([carriage_length, carriage_width, carriage_height], center=true);
    rotate([90,0,0]) cylinder(h=carriage_width+2, d=screw_clearance_diameter, center=true, $fn=64);
  }

Rule: lead nut pocket is a larger recess around screw axis.
OpenSCAD action:
  rotate([90,0,0]) cylinder(h=nut_pocket_depth, d=lead_nut_outer_diameter+clearance, center=true, $fn=6);

Rule: guide rod holes are parallel to screw axis.
OpenSCAD action:
  for (x=[-guide_spacing/2, guide_spacing/2])
    translate([x,0,0]) rotate([90,0,0]) cylinder(h=carriage_width+2, d=guide_rod_diameter+clearance, center=true, $fn=64);

Rule: motor mount has shaft opening plus bolt pattern.
OpenSCAD action:
  cylinder(h=motor_plate_thickness+2, d=motor_shaft_clearance, center=true, $fn=96);
  for (x=[-motor_bolt_spacing/2,motor_bolt_spacing/2])
    for (z=[-motor_bolt_spacing/2,motor_bolt_spacing/2])
      translate([x,0,z]) rotate([90,0,0]) cylinder(h=motor_plate_thickness+2, d=motor_bolt_diameter, center=true, $fn=48);

Rule: end bearing support has coaxial bearing seat and screw clearance.
OpenSCAD action:
  rotate([90,0,0]) cylinder(h=support_width+2, d=bearing_outer_diameter, center=true, $fn=96);
