WHEEL TYPES
────────────
1. Solid Disc Wheel — flat disc, simple; heavy, no compliance
2. Spoke Wheel — hub + spokes + rim; lighter, printed-friendly
3. O-Ring Tyre Wheel — rubber o-ring in groove; good traction, compliant
4. TPU Tyre Wheel — flexible filament overmould or separate tyre
5. Mecanum Wheel — angled rollers allow omnidirectional motion
6. Omni Wheel (roller)  — radial rollers for sideways motion
7. Pneumatic / Inflatable — RC car tyre; foam or air-filled
8. Swivel Castor — wheel in yoke that pivots 360° for free rolling

SPOKE WHEEL PARAMETERS
────────────────────────
  wheel_od              — outer diameter of rim
  wheel_width           — width of wheel (tyre contact width)
  hub_od                — central hub outer diameter
  hub_bore              — shaft bore diameter
  spoke_count           — 3–8 spokes; odd numbers avoid aligned stress points
  spoke_width           — 4–8 mm (thicker = stronger)
  spoke_thickness       — 3–5 mm
  rim_thickness         — 3–6 mm
  rim_width             — wheel_width (full width)
  tyre_groove_d         — o_ring_cord_d × 0.8 (slight compression for retention)
  tyre_groove_depth     — o_ring_cord_d × 0.5 (half-buried)

  module spoke_wheel(od=80, width=20, hub_od=20, bore_d=6, n_spokes=5) {
    rim_t = 4;
    spoke_w = 5;
    difference() {
      union() {
        // rim
        difference() {
          cylinder(h=width, d=od, center=true, $fn=128);
          cylinder(h=width+2, d=od-2*rim_t, center=true, $fn=128);
        }
        // hub
        cylinder(h=width, d=hub_od, center=true, $fn=64);
        // spokes
        for(i=[0:n_spokes-1])
          rotate([0, 0, 360*i/n_spokes])
            translate([(hub_od/2 + (od/2-rim_t-hub_od/2)/2), 0, 0])
              cube([od/2-rim_t-hub_od/2, spoke_w, width-2], center=true);
      }
      // bore
      cylinder(h=width+2, d=bore_d+0.2, center=true, $fn=64);
    }
  }

O-RING TYRE DIMENSIONS (standard o-ring cord diameters)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  o_ring sizes for tyres:
  cord ø2.0 mm → wheel groove ø: wheel_od − 2×1.0 mm (sits proud 1 mm)
  cord ø2.5 mm → groove depth 1.25 mm, groove width 2.6 mm
  cord ø3.0 mm → groove depth 1.5 mm, groove width 3.1 mm
  cord ø4.0 mm → groove depth 2.0 mm, groove width 4.2 mm
  cord ø5.0 mm → groove depth 2.5 mm, groove width 5.2 mm

  Common o-ring tyre sizes for robots:
  Wheel OD 40 mm: o-ring 38 × 2 mm (ID 38, cord 2)
  Wheel OD 60 mm: o-ring 56 × 3 mm
  Wheel OD 80 mm: o-ring 75 × 3 mm or 76 × 4 mm
  Wheel OD 100 mm: o-ring 95 × 4 mm

MECANUM WHEEL PARAMETERS
──────────────────────────
  wheel_od              — overall diameter
  roller_count          — 8–12 rollers (more = smoother)
  roller_angle          — 45° (standard mecanum)
  roller_od             — wheel_od × 0.35 approximately
  roller_length         — wheel_width × 0.7
  roller_spacing_angle  — 360° / roller_count
  hub_od                — ≥ bore_d + 12 mm
  roller_pin_d          — 3–4 mm M3 shaft or shoulder bolt

  Kinematics: FL = FR = RL = RR = pure forward
              FL = -FR = RL = -RR = pure rotation
              FL = RL = 0, FR = RR = v → pure sideways

CASTOR WHEEL ASSEMBLY
──────────────────────
  wheel_od              — 30–75 mm
  wheel_width           — 15–30 mm
  yoke_width            — wheel_width + 4 mm
  yoke_height           — wheel_od/2 + 20 mm (clears ground)
  swivel_plate_d        — 35–70 mm
  swivel_bore_d         — 8–12 mm (ball bearing or plain)
  swivel_bolt_circle    — swivel_plate_d × 0.7
  n_swivel_bolts        — 3 or 4
  offset_distance       — 15–25 mm (wheel centre behind swivel axis for self-centering)

GROUND CLEARANCE GUIDE (mobile robots)
────────────────────────────────────────
  Indoor flat floor: 5–10 mm clearance
  Uneven carpet: 10–20 mm
  Outdoor smooth: 20–30 mm
  Rough terrain: 40–60 mm
  chassis_ground_clearance = wheel_radius - (axle_height_from_ground)

DESIGN RULES
────────────
  - Spoke count: 3 minimum (stability); 5 or 7 (odd) distributes stress better than even
  - O-ring tyre: wheel groove width = o_ring_cord_d + 0.2 mm; print grove in full round
  - Mecanum roller angle must be EXACTLY 45° for correct kinematics; use rotate([0,0,45])
  - Castor offset: wheel centre BEHIND swivel axis; otherwise castor oscillates (shimmy)
  - For differential-drive robots: wheelbase / track ratio 0.8–1.2 for good turning
  - Print wheels standing on rim edge for best layer orientation (spoke loads go through layers)

OPENSCAD CODE INTERPRETATION
Mechanical meaning to code:
- A wheel is a rotating disk with hub, rim, tire, axle bore, and optional spokes.
- Rim, hub, tire band, spokes, and castor fork are solid material in union().
- Axle bore, bearing seats, lightening holes, and fork clearance are removed material in difference().
- Wheel axis is usually X or Z; choose Z for standalone wheel unless assembly requires horizontal axle.
- Spokes are repeated rectangular or hull-based solids between hub and rim.
- Tire groove/tread can be approximated by repeated shallow cuts around the rim.
- Castor fork has two side plates with an axle hole through both plates.
- Castor swivel uses a vertical bore or pin above the fork, offset ahead of the wheel center if requested.

OPENSCAD CODE RULE IMPLEMENTATIONS
Use these patterns to turn wheel, tire, and castor rules into OpenSCAD.

Rule: wheel rim, hub, and tire band are coaxial solids.
OpenSCAD action:
  union() {
    cylinder(h=wheel_width, d=wheel_diameter, center=true, $fn=128);
    cylinder(h=hub_width, d=hub_diameter, center=true, $fn=96);
    cylinder(h=tire_width, d=tire_outer_diameter, center=true, $fn=128);
  }

Rule: axle bore passes through wheel center.
OpenSCAD action:
  cylinder(h=hub_width+2, d=axle_diameter+clearance, center=true, $fn=96);

Rule: spokes repeat between hub and rim.
OpenSCAD action:
  for (i=[0:spoke_count-1])
    rotate([0,0,360*i/spoke_count])
      translate([(hub_diameter+wheel_diameter)/4,0,0]) cube([(wheel_diameter-hub_diameter)/2, spoke_width, wheel_width], center=true);

Rule: tread grooves are repeated shallow cuts around tire.
OpenSCAD action:
  for (i=[0:tread_count-1])
    rotate([0,0,360*i/tread_count])
      translate([tire_outer_diameter/2,0,0]) cube([tread_depth, tread_width, tire_width+2], center=true);

Rule: castor fork has two side plates and axle holes.
OpenSCAD action:
  for (y=[-fork_gap/2-side_plate_thickness/2, fork_gap/2+side_plate_thickness/2])
    translate([0,y,0]) cube([fork_length, side_plate_thickness, fork_height], center=true);
  for (y=[-fork_gap/2-side_plate_thickness/2, fork_gap/2+side_plate_thickness/2])
    translate([0,y,axle_z]) rotate([90,0,0]) cylinder(h=side_plate_thickness+2, d=axle_diameter+clearance, center=true, $fn=48);
